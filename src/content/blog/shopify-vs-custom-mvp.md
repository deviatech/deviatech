We get this question a lot: "Should I use Shopify, WooCommerce, or build something custom?" The honest answer is usually the least complicated option that supports the way you sell today. For many Pakistani product businesses, that means Shopify or WooCommerce, not a custom platform.

## Shopify: best when speed and managed operations matter

- You are selling clothing, food, gifts, beauty products, or other products with a standard buying flow.
- You need to launch in days rather than spend months maintaining a custom build.
- You want product, discount, and order management without maintaining the server and application yourself.
- You can work within Shopify's payment, app, theme, and checkout constraints.

For Pakistan, confirm the payment and COD setup before committing. Shopify can support a strong storefront, but the exact gateway, settlement, courier, and tax workflow still needs to be checked for your business.

## WooCommerce: best when WordPress ownership matters

WooCommerce can make sense when you already operate a WordPress site, need control over hosting and plugins, or have a team comfortable with WordPress maintenance. It can be flexible, but that flexibility means someone owns updates, backups, security, plugin conflicts, payment changes, and performance.

The common mistake is comparing only the initial build quote. Include hosting, premium plugins, maintenance time, payment integration work, backups, and the cost of fixing a plugin conflict when comparing WooCommerce with Shopify.

## Custom software: best when the workflow is the product

- Your core value is a workflow Shopify or WooCommerce cannot support without awkward workarounds, such as a marketplace, a complex subscription rule, or multi-vendor logistics.
- You need a product, not just a catalogue: users need to complete a workflow that is the actual business value.
- You have an owner who can make scope decisions and a budget for continued maintenance.

## A simple cost comparison

These are planning ranges, not quotes:

- **Shopify:** DeviaTech store projects start from PKR 40,000, before Shopify subscription, domain, paid apps, provider fees, and marketing.
- **WooCommerce:** the build may look comparable at first, but hosting, premium plugins, security, backups, and ongoing maintenance should be budgeted separately.
- **Custom software:** focused MVP or workflow projects start from PKR 150,000 and can rise with roles, integrations, design, data migration, and testing.

If a standard buying flow can get you to your next meaningful milestone, custom software is usually premature. If the workflow is the reason customers choose you, the cheaper-looking platform can become expensive through workarounds.

## FAQ

### Is Shopify or WooCommerce better for COD?

Neither platform makes COD operations automatic. The important questions are whether your checkout captures the information you need, how orders reach fulfilment, and how returns and failed deliveries are handled.

### Can I start with Shopify and migrate later?

Yes, but migration has costs. Keep product data, customer permissions, URLs, order history, analytics, and redirects in mind before treating migration as effortless.

### When should a startup avoid Shopify?

Avoid forcing a product workflow into Shopify when the core value is not standard product checkout. Start with a focused MVP when you need to test a custom user journey.
