Before running ads to a Shopify store in Pakistan, complete a real customer journey from product page to delivered-order operation. The goal is not only that checkout technically works. The goal is that your team can confirm, dispatch, track, and support the order while your analytics records what happened.

## COD checklist

- Capture a usable phone number and address.
- Define who confirms the order and how quickly.
- Record what happens when the customer does not answer.
- Decide how failed deliveries, exchanges, and returns are tracked.
- Make sure the person packing orders can see the information they need.

## Payment checklist

Confirm provider eligibility, settlement details, currency, checkout behaviour, failed-payment handling, refund steps, and reconciliation. Payment availability changes by provider and business setup, so confirm the current requirements with the provider before building the promise into your store.

## Shipping checklist

Write the dispatch window, delivery coverage, charges, and exception process. Check whether the courier collects from your area, how tracking is shared, and what happens to an order with an incomplete address. A nationwide shipping claim is only useful if your operation can fulfil it.

## Meta Pixel checklist

Verify page view, product view, add to cart, checkout, and purchase events in the real mobile flow. Test a successful and failed payment separately. Watch for duplicate events, purchase events firing before payment success, and missing product or value data.

## The most common launch mistake

Teams install tracking, add a COD option, and publish the store without placing an order the way a real customer will. Use mobile data, a real phone, the actual notification channel, and the actual fulfilment process.

## FAQ

### Should every Pakistani store offer COD?

Not automatically. COD may be important for your audience, but its costs and failed-delivery risk need to fit your category, margins, and fulfilment capacity.

### Can Meta Pixel fix a weak checkout?

No. Pixel data can show where customers drop off, but it cannot repair unclear shipping, a failed payment flow, or slow customer confirmation.
