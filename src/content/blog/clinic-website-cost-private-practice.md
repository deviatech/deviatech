A clinic website is usually less expensive to build when it explains your services and sends enquiries to your team. Costs usually rise when it needs to confirm appointments online, coordinate several practitioners or locations, or publish content in multiple languages. Compare **the one-off build, recurring services and optional ongoing support** as separate figures before choosing a quote.

This guide is for therapists, psychology practices and private clinics in Pakistan and Europe. It shows what to put in a brief and how to compare quotes that may cover different work.

## Compare three ways to handle appointments

| Approach | What patients can do | What the quote should cover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phone or WhatsApp | Contact your team, who check availability and arrange the appointment. | Clear contact information, working links and mobile testing. No calendar integration. |
| Appointment request form | Send contact details and a preferred time for your team to review. | Form setup, delivery to the right inbox, error handling and testing. |
| Online scheduling | Choose an available slot and receive confirmation under your booking tool's rules. | Scheduling setup, practitioner calendars, appointment rules and testing. List provider charges separately. |

**A request is not a confirmed appointment.** If your staff need to review a form submission, the button and success message should say so. Ask only for what staff need to respond, not clinical details.

If you already use a booking service, linking to it usually takes less work than embedding and configuring it. Ask for a separate line item for calendar sync, reminders, cancellation rules, time zones or payments if you need them. A custom booking system is a separate project, not something to assume is included when a proposal says “booking page”.

## What else changes the build price?

**Pages and content.** One services page with a short overview is different from six service pages that need writing, images and review. List the pages and agree who supplies service descriptions, fees, practitioner bios, photos, practical information and policy wording. If you are replacing a site, flag the content that needs moving and the pages that need redirects. Your practice should approve clinical statements and credentials.

**Design and editing.** Adapting a suitable design can reduce layout work. A new visual direction, custom illustrations and more revisions add time. Ask what you can update yourself. Editing opening hours is simpler than managing a team directory, publishing articles or keeping several languages in sync. If editing tools are included, clarify who gets access and whether training is part of handover.

**Languages and locations.** In Pakistan, ask whether English meets your patients' needs or whether Urdu would help. In Europe, identify the specific country and languages you need. Each additional language means translation, review and layout checks. Extra locations can mean separate hours, contact details and service pages.

**Testing and launch.** Check whether the quote includes mobile and keyboard checks, form or scheduling tests, and launch support. If it says “SEO included”, ask for the actual deliverables, such as page titles, headings and a sitemap, rather than treating that phrase as a promise of rankings.

## Separate the build from running costs

| Cost category | What to ask for |
| --- | --- |
| One-off build | Design, development, content work, integrations, testing and launch support. |
| Domain, hosting and email | Renewal prices, who owns the accounts, and any charges for inboxes or form delivery. |
| Scheduling or payments | Provider plan, practitioner limits, reminder charges and transaction fees, if applicable. |
| Maintenance | Who handles updates, monitoring and backups where applicable, and which fixes are included. |
| Future changes | Separate pricing for new pages, translations, photos and changes outside the original scope. |

Ask for the **first-year total and expected renewal-year total**. For a like-for-like comparison, add the build fee to the first year of required services and any support you choose. For the renewal year, list only charges you expect to continue. Estimate usage charges on the same assumptions in both quotes, such as the number of reminder messages. Confirm the quote currency, whether tax is included, and when payments are due. If you pay across currencies, ask who covers conversion or payment fees. Agree what you'll receive at handover and what you can take with you if you change providers.

## Example: one practice, two possible quotes

Imagine a fictional solo counselling practice with one location, approved English copy and existing photos. Its receptionist already arranges appointments. The first quote could cover five pages, phone and WhatsApp links, and a simple enquiry form. The developer adapts the design, adds the supplied content, tests the contact routes and launches the site.

Now consider the same practice after it adds three practitioners, a second language and online scheduling. The new quote needs profiles, translation work, calendar rules and a larger testing scope. Some of those changes add to the build; a booking-provider subscription may add to the running cost.

If you're not sure whether your practice needs scheduling, ask for the simpler site and a separately priced scheduling option. That lets you decide whether letting patients book online is worth the added setup and subscription.

## What to send when asking for a quote

A short brief is enough to start. Include:

1. **Your practice:** country, locations, services and number of practitioners.
2. **Your patients:** which languages they need and whether appointments are in person, online or both.
3. **Pages:** what you need at launch and what should move from your current site.
4. **Appointments:** how patients should contact you or book, any existing scheduling tool, and who manages requests.
5. **Content:** what is ready and whether you need writing, translation or photography.
6. **Budget and timing:** preferred quote currency, a budget range if you have one, and any launch deadline.

Leave out patient records and confidential clinical details. For a fuller planning list, see the [clinic website checklist for private practices](/blog/clinic-website-checklist-private-practice).
