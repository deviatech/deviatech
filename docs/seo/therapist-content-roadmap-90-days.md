# Therapist service — 90-day content roadmap

Created 2026-09-17. This is a research and planning document. **Nothing
here is approved for publication.** Each item needs the owner's sign-off
before it is written, and no article should be published without a named
reviewer and verified sources.

## Basis and its limits

This roadmap is built from the intent themes in
`THERAPIST-SEO-TECHNICAL-SPEC.md` §4 and from the service's actual buyer
questions, because **Search Console had no query data available when this
was written** — the property was not yet accessible from this session.

That is a real limitation. Topic priorities below are informed judgement,
not measured demand. Once Search Console accumulates four to six weeks of
query data, revisit the priority column and let real queries override
these assumptions. Do not treat the ordering as validated.

## Ground rules

- No city or country doorway pages. Location is mentioned only where it is
  truthful and useful to the reader.
- No medical, clinical, or diagnostic advice. Every article stays on the
  web-design and business-operations side of the line, and says so where
  the boundary could blur.
- No fabricated statistics, client counts, case studies, or testimonials.
  A claim either carries a source or does not appear.
- No guaranteed-ranking, guaranteed-leads, or "best/#1" language.
- Persian and Urdu versions are native-quality localizations or they are
  not published. Machine translation is not acceptable.
- Every article links to its supporting landing page with natural anchor
  text, and the landing is never keyword-stuffed to accommodate it.

## Cannibalization policy

The three landing pages own the transactional intent for
"therapist website design" and its close variants in each language.
Articles target informational intent only. If an article starts ranking
for a transactional query the landing should own, treat that as a defect:
retitle the article, strengthen its internal link to the landing, or merge
it. Check for this at each monthly review.

## Sequencing and capacity

Nine articles in ninety days is roughly one every ten days, which is
realistic only if the sales pilot is not consuming the same hours. If it
is, cut the roadmap rather than the quality: publishing 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3
well beats publishing all nine thinly.

Localize into Persian and Urdu only after the English version has been
reviewed and published. A localized version of a weak article multiplies
the weakness.

## Review triggers

- After four to six weeks of Search Console query data, re-order by
  measured demand and drop topics with no observed interest.
- If any article ranks for a query the landing should own, treat it as
  cannibalization and fix it that month.
- If the sales pilot surfaces a question prospects actually ask that is
  not on this list, that question outranks everything here.
